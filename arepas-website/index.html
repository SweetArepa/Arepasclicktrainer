    Reset

    Nylocas demi-boss

    • The boss shows a style: white melee, blue magic, green ranged. It starts on melee and changes to a random different style every 10 seconds.
    • To hit it correctly you need all the gear of its colour and both of its prayers: melee = Protect from Melee + Piety · ranged = Protect from Missiles + Rigour · magic = Protect from Magic + Augury.
    • Click the boss to start attacking. You then hit it automatically with your weapon (the first box of each colour): melee and ranged every 3.0s, magic every 1.8s. Swapping gear or prayers keeps you attacking; clicking the floor stops.
    • Your switch time is from the colour change to your first correct hit. Hits in the wrong gear or prayers count as wrong-setup hits. A colour with no correct hit is a missed phase.

    Setting up

    • Press Edit layout (P). Drag the boss box and the inventory where they sit on your screen, and resize both from their orange corners.
    • On the Inv tab, click slots to set your gear: empty → white → blue → green. On the Pray tab, drag prayers to match your in-game order.
    • Save it all as a setup with Save as….

    Keys

    • Tab hotkeys default to F1 inventory, F2 equipment, F3 prayer, F4 magic. Rebind them in Settings.
    • Enter start · Esc stop · P edit layout · H hide controls · ` panel

    Click trainer

    • Classic: click the gold boxes as fast as you can (no inventory on screen).
    • Swap: click the highlighted gear boxes (white melee, blue magic, green ranged). Each one equips like in game.
    • Cast: open the magic tab (hotkey), click Ice Barrage, then click the blue box.
    • Mixed: a random mix of the three.

    Scoring

    • Every required click is 1 point, or 2 points if it lands within 600ms (one tick) of becoming due. A miss-click costs 1 point.
    • In swaps and casts, each click's clock starts when the previous one lands.
    • Boxes stay up until you hit them. Clicking a tab never counts as a miss.
    • Timed runs keep your best score and your last 5 runs for each mode and timer. Older runs are deleted.

    Setup

    1. Set Client to your RuneLite game size (Configuration → RuneLite → Game size), or drag the frame's edges. Keep browser zoom at 100% (⌘0).
    2. Take a screenshot of just the game area during a Maiden spawn, from your usual standing spot and camera angle. Screenshot… loads it as the background, and the client resizes to match (Retina screenshots are halved automatically).
    3. Click Place boxes, then drag each box onto its crab. Drag the orange corner to resize it.
    4. Line the frame up with where RuneLite sits on your screen: go fullscreen, press H to hide the controls, and in Place mode drag empty space or use the arrow keys.
    5. Press Save as… to keep it as a named setup. Switch between setups from the Setup menu.

    Easiest 1:1 match: take a full-screen screenshot while playing (⇧⌘3), load it, and move the frame to 0, 0.

    Rules

    • Each round is about one crab: the top priority of the ones that spawned, in the order S1 → S2 → N1 → N2.
    • Ice Rush is queued when the crabs spawn. Your first click uses it up, and it does not come back.
    • S2 on top: click S2 once, within 0.6s of the crabs appearing. The Rush lands 0.6s after your click. Any other click inside that 0.6s overrides it and you fail.
    • S1 / N1 / N2 on top: click the floor (or S2) to cancel the Rush, then click the crab. Both clicks must land within 0.6s of the crabs appearing. The crab gets Ice Barrage.
    • The round ends when a spell lands on a crab. A slow second click still ends the round, then fails it with the time shown.
